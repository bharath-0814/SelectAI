Downloaded the 69 MB Zip? Here's What's Next

SelectAI is 100% standalone. No installers, no runtime setup, no admin privileges required.

01

Extract the Zip

Right-click SelectAI-v1.0.0-win-x64.zip and choose "Extract All..." to any folder (e.g. your Desktop or Downloads).

02

Double-Click App

Open the folder and run SelectAI.exe. If Windows SmartScreen shows a blue banner, tap "More info" → "Run anyway".

03

Tray Docking

SelectAI starts quietly and docks in your Windows system tray (bottom-right near clock) with a glowing cyan circle icon.

04

Press Hotkey

Press Ctrl + Shift + Space anywhere in Windows to freeze the screen, circle content, and Ask AI!
